- **Tech & Methods:** `Python` · `NumPy` · `Pygame`. Benchmarked Q-Learning, SARSA, and Monte Carlo algorithms for optimal convergence under stochastic wave/wind drift.

#### • Urban Data Lake & Automated Ingestion Pipeline (Madrid)
- **Focus:** Event-driven microservices architecture for municipal environmental metrics and ETL processing.
- **Tech & Methods:** `RabbitMQ` · `n8n` · `PostgreSQL` · `MinIO (S3)` · `Apache Superset`.

#### • Metaheuristic Combinatorial Optimization (TSP & PID)
- **Focus:** High-performance heuristic optimization engine designed for route planning across Madrid and automated controller adaptation.
- **Tech & Methods:** `Python` · `Optuna` · `NumPy`. Genetic Algorithms with adaptive crossover/mutation operators and 2-Opt local search refinement.

#### • Distributed Polyglot NoSQL Architecture
- **Focus:** Multi-model database design migrating relational schemas to specialized high-throughput storage engines.
- **Tech & Methods:** `MongoDB` (document model), `Cassandra` (partitioned leaderboards), `Redis` (vector embeddings/caching), and `Neo4j` (Graph algorithms: PageRank, Leiden community detection).
